Trailing (ttm GAAP) EPS is often distorted by one-off charges - M&A amortization, stock-based comp, impairments - which can make the raw ratio explode into a number unrelated to the business's real growth (e.g. a name with acquisition-related amortization depressing its trailing EPS can show 200%+ "growth" that is really just a low base, not performance).

Limitations & disclaimer

  • Data is pulled from Yahoo Finance via yfinance at run time; Yahoo's data can lag, be revised, or occasionally be unavailable/rate-limited for a given ticker — any such gap is flagged as "N/A", never invented.
  • The valuation benchmark is an in-house peer median from the curated universe in this repo, not a professional third-party index (e.g. no Bloomberg/FactSet/Damodaran subscription was used).
  • Price targets are estimates built on two disclosed, simplified, peer-relative methodologies (forward P/E percentile; PEG-ratio percentile) — they are not guarantees, and are only as good as the peer group and the consensus forward estimates they're anchored to. Both methods are relative-value, not intrinsic-value: if an entire peer group is overvalued, both methods will say so too. A prior version used a discounted cash-flow (DCF) method instead of PEG; it was dropped because its terminal value is highly sensitive to the (small) gap between the discount rate and the terminal growth rate, which produced misleading "bull case below current price" results for richly-valued, debt-carrying names.
  • Moat/competitive-advantage assessments and the "Business overview" (segments, customers, key risks) are qualitative analyst opinion grounded in publicly known business characteristics, not derived from the live data pull; they are compiled only for names that have appeared in the selected top 10, not the full 50-name universe.
  • Entry zones and stop-losses are derived mechanically from recent price history (50-day moving average, 14-day ATR) — they are not predictions and do not account for upcoming earnings/news catalysts.
  • Fee, FX-shock, and fractional-share assumptions are illustrative; confirm actual costs and fractional-share availability with your own broker before trading.
  • The backtest uses today's screened basket applied retroactively — it has obvious hindsight/look-ahead bias (the names were chosen because their historical growth/valuation looked good) and is not evidence the screening method would have selected these same names in the past, nor a guarantee of future results.
  • The 5 forward scenarios are mechanical, formula-driven projections (peer forward-P/E and PEG percentiles, a fixed FX shock size), not probability-weighted forecasts; no probability is assigned to any scenario, and real outcomes will differ from all five.
  • This is educational research, not personalised financial advice. It does not account for your full financial situation, tax treatment, or any positions you already hold. Final investment decisions — including whether to invest at all — are yours alone.
