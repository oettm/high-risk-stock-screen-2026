Trailing (ttm GAAP) EPS is often distorted by one-off charges - M&A amortization, stock-based comp, impairments - which can make the raw ratio explode into a number unrelated to the business's real growth.

Limitations & disclaimer

  • Data is pulled from Yahoo Finance via yfinance at run time; any gap is flagged as "N/A", never invented.
  • The valuation benchmark (sector peer median forward P/E) and risk-rating percentiles are computed against the same 50-name curated universe used in the main screener, not a third-party index.
  • Price targets are estimates built on two disclosed, peer-relative methodologies (forward P/E percentile; PEG-ratio percentile) — both relative-value, not intrinsic-value, methods. A prior version used a DCF instead of PEG; dropped for its terminal-value sensitivity producing misleading results on richly-valued, debt-carrying names - see the main report's limitations for the full rationale.
  • The Sharpe ratio uses the US 13-week T-bill yield (^IRX) as its risk-free proxy - a live, free, no-key series, but not a precise match for a EUR-based investor. Treat it as directionally informative, not a precision instrument.
  • Moat/competitive-advantage assessments and the "Business overview" are qualitative analyst opinion grounded in publicly known business characteristics, not derived from the live data pull.
  • Entry zones and stop-losses are derived mechanically from recent price history — not predictions, and do not account for upcoming earnings/news catalysts.
  • The whole-share allocation algorithm is a greedy heuristic, not a guaranteed-optimal solution to matching target weights - with only 8 names and a few hundred to low-thousands of euros per name, small rounding gaps versus the 12.5% target are normal and shown explicitly (see "deviation" column).
  • The backtest is a historical look-back on hand-picked names, not a forecast; the 5 forward scenarios are mechanical, formula-driven projections, not probability-weighted forecasts - no probability is assigned to any of the five, and real outcomes will differ from all of them.
